    Abstract :

    A transcription of the text of John in Papyrus 75 (Vatican, Biblioteca Apostolica Vaticana) produced by members of the International Greek New Testament Project working towards the Novum Testamentum Graecum - Editio Critica Maior edition of the Gospel according to John.


    The current version of this file in the online edition appears at: http://www.iohannes.com/XML/transcriptions/greek/04_P75.xml
